# Characters

## Meera Rao
- **Role:** Protagonist.
- **Age/look:** 28. Short hair she cuts herself, oversized shirts, a plastic watch, scooty helmet with a sticker peeling off. From Ep 1 to Ep 10 her left wrist is in a blue cast.
- **Want:** To need nobody, and to keep things with Karthik "easy" and unnamed.
- **Need:** To admit she chose Karthik long ago, and to ask out loud to be chosen back.
- **Fear / wound:** Her father died suddenly when she was 19. Her mother fell apart, and Meera became the one who coped. Asking for help feels like being a burden.
- **Secret:** She has never told her mother Karthik exists. Her phone's favourites list has one name on it: his.
- **Arc:** Proud self-sufficiency ("he's not my boyfriend") → shaken when she learns who wrote her emergency form → tries the "safe", labelled option with Arjun → sees both kinds of half-love clearly → turns both men down and chooses herself → asks for the whole thing on her own terms and hands Karthik the pen.
- **Voice:** Dry, quick, deflects with jokes, goes very quiet when hurt. Hyderabad Telugu with English mixed in. *"Nenu okkate manage chesukuntanu. Always chesukunna kada."*
- **Relationships:** Karthik (unnamed love), Arjun (ex who proposed), Padma (mother she protects), Jyothi (the nurse who sees everything).

## Karthik
- **Role:** The situationship and love interest.
- **Age/look:** 30. Sound engineer at a Madhapur studio. Headphones always round his neck, rolled sleeves, a steel water bottle. Calm hands.
- **Want:** To keep Meera without the label he thinks breaks things.
- **Need:** To learn that refusing to name a love is its own way of leaving.
- **Fear / wound:** His father left his mother Sarala after twenty years of marriage. Karthik was 14 and watched her wait by the gate for months. Since then he believes naming something is how you lose it.
- **Secret:** He has kept every voice note Meera ever sent, in a folder on his laptop called "work".
- **Arc:** Dependable but evasive ("someone from work") → walks out when asked directly → falls apart when his mother is admitted, and calls Meera → freezes over his mother's form → loses Meera → chooses to say it out loud, on speaker, in front of witnesses.
- **Voice:** Few words, practical, warm through actions. Answers questions with questions. *"Neeku water kaavala? … Mundu taagu. Taruvatha matladudam."*
- **Relationships:** Meera, his mother Sarala (devoted, protective), Arjun (barely knows him; quiet tension).

## Arjun Varma
- **Role:** The ex. A real alternative, not a villain.
- **Age/look:** 31. Strategy consultant, crisp shirts, two phones, a smartwatch that buzzes all the time.
- **Want:** A second chance and the future he planned: wedding in Margasira, a flat in Kondapur.
- **Need:** To accept the truth he already wrote down seven months ago, and to let go with dignity.
- **Fear / wound:** Being the "reliable son" who does everything right and still isn't enough.
- **Secret:** He wrote Karthik's number on Meera's admission form the night she had dengue. He broke up with her a week later and never told her why.
- **Arc:** Returns, softened and honest → offers commitment again → shows the same absence at the date and the family lunch → hears Meera's "no" → takes a transfer to Pune and sends a gracious goodbye.
- **Voice:** Articulate, plans out loud, English-heavy Telugu, calls her "Meeru" from their old days. *"Nenu anni plan chesanu, Meeru. Nuvvu just yes cheppu."*
- **Relationships:** Meera, his mother Lakshmi (pushes for the match), Padma (adores him).

## Sarala
- **Role:** Karthik's mother. The key to his fear.
- **Age/look:** 56. Retired government schoolteacher in Warangal, cotton sarees, reading glasses on a chain.
- **Want:** To see her son unafraid.
- **Need:** To tell the truth about her marriage, which she has softened for years.
- **Fear / wound:** That her waiting at the gate taught her son that love leaves.
- **Secret:** She has known about "the girl Karthik talks to every night" for a year.
- **Arc:** A voice on the phone (Eps 1, 5) → admitted to hospital with chest pain (Ep 6) → tells Meera the story and asks for the girl's number on her form (Ep 7) → her call becomes the moment Karthik finally says it (Ep 10).
- **Voice:** Gentle, teacherly, sees through people. *"Peru pedithe pogoduthundi anukuntadu vaadu. Peru pettakapothe ekkuva pogoduthundi ani teliyadu."*
- **Relationships:** Karthik (only child), Meera (instant, quiet alliance).

## Jyothi
- **Role:** Night-shift nurse at Aarogya Hospital. Chorus and witness.
- **Age/look:** 40s. Practical, unsentimental, sees a hundred couples a week.
- **Want:** Her ward to run smoothly. Secretly, for this one to work out.
- **Arc:** Asks "Do you want him to leave?" (Ep 1) → reveals the form (Ep 2) → is at reception for the update in Ep 10: "So… boyfriend?"
- **Voice:** Flat, kind, no time for nonsense. *"Madam, pain ki tablet ivvagalanu. Confusion ki ivvalenu."*

## Padma Rao
- **Role:** Meera's mother in Vijayawada, heard mostly on the phone.
- **Age/look:** 55. Widow, music teacher.
- **Want:** Meera "settled", ideally with Arjun, whom she adores.
- **Arc:** Never told about the accident (Ep 2) → excited about the family lunch (Eps 8-9) → hears her daughter say no, and quietly backs her (Ep 9).
- **Voice:** Affectionate and anxious. *"Nuvvu anni okkate chesukuntav. Konchem amma ki cheppu, Meeru."*

## Lakshmi Varma
- **Role:** Arjun's mother (Ep 9).
- **Voice:** Brisk, warm, already choosing wedding dates. *"Margasira lo manchi muhurtham undi, Padma garu!"*

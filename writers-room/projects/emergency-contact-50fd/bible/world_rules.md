# World rules

## Setting and period
Present-day Hyderabad, monsoon season (July–August). Gachibowli and Madhapur flats, Kondapur cafés, the Biodiversity flyover in the rain. Episodes 6–7 move to Warangal: an older town, a district hospital, Sarala's house with a front gate. The ten episodes cover about three weeks, from the accident to the cast coming off.

## The social world and its rules
- **Situationships are normal but unspoken.** Friends know about Meera and Karthik; family does not. Saying "boyfriend" to family means marriage talk, so many couples just don't.
- **Mothers are the pressure system.** Weddings are negotiated between mothers (Padma and Lakshmi) before the couple has decided. That pressure is affectionate, not villainous.
- **Hospital procedure is real and drives the plot.** Aarogya Hospital keeps a patient's emergency contact on file from earlier admissions. On a new admission the duty nurse calls the number on file. A form shows the name, the relationship ("friend") and the signature of whoever filled it. Meera's file is from her dengue admission seven months ago, filled and signed by Arjun Varma.
- **Phones are the battlefield.** Who you call, whose call you take on speaker, what someone is saved as, voice notes. Characters reveal themselves through phone behaviour.
- **Independence is a value and a wall.** Meera is admired for handling everything alone. The story questions that admiration but never shames her for it.

## Genre contract and tone
Restrained, bittersweet romance. No villains, no slaps, no screaming, no slow-motion rain dance. Emotion arrives through pauses, small acts of care (water bottles, a cut-up mango, a held charger) and things almost said. Every episode still needs a sharp hook and a concrete turn: restraint is not slowness. The ending is hopeful but earned and incomplete. Meera says "Inka decide cheyyaledu".

## Visual motifs
- **The emergency contact form:** blank lines, a hovering pen, handwriting.
- **The chair beside a bed:** Karthik asleep in it (Ep 1); Meera asleep in it in Warangal (Ep 7).
- **The blue cast**, which collects signatures. Karthik never signs it until Ep 10.
- **Phone screens:** "Karthik ❤" changed to "Karthik" and back again; "Amma calling…"
- **Rain** on windows at every turning point.
- **The gate** at Sarala's house, where she once waited.

## Language
Dialogue is natural, conversational spoken Telugu in the Hyderabad urban register (vyavaharika), with English words where people really use them (hospital, sorry, okay, boyfriend, office, form, plan). English words are written in Latin script inside the Telugu line. Scene headings, action and parentheticals are in English.

## Source vs invention
- Hyderabad and Warangal geography, monsoon setting, hospital admission procedure — SOURCE (real-world observation; contemporary Hyderabad life).
- Telugu family and marriage customs (muhurtham in Margasira, mothers arranging the first lunch) — SOURCE (contemporary Telugu middle-class practice).
- Aarogya Hospital, all characters, the dengue admission and the form — INVENTION.
- Karthik's father leaving after twenty years — INVENTION, the emotional engine of his fear.

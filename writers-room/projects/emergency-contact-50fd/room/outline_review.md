# Maya, Emotional Architect

## Verdict
The emotional spine is strong: a woman who never asks, between a man who never says and a man who never stays. Episodes 1, 6 and 7 earn their feeling, because care is shown before it is questioned. But Karthik's love goes invisible in Ep 5 and Ep 8, and the Ep 10 payoff leans on a change of heart we never watch happen.

## Issues
1. **HIGH, Ep 5.** "Nee istam" plays as indifference, so a viewer has no reason to keep hoping for Karthik. *Fix:* keep the line, but let the camera catch what Meera doesn't see: he burns the pesarattu, or his knuckles whiten on the pan, after he reads Arjun's message. We need to know he is hurting.
2. **HIGH, Ep 8 → 10.** Between putting the pen down (Ep 8) and naming her on speaker (Ep 10), Karthik has no on-screen turning point. The payoff looks sudden. *Fix:* give him one private beat in Ep 9 or the Ep 9 ending, an act that costs him, before the public declaration.
3. **MEDIUM, Ep 2.** Meera lying to her mother is a lovely plant ("she copes alone"), but it never pays off. *Fix:* let Padma discover the accident herself in Ep 9, and be hurt that she wasn't told. That mirrors the emergency-contact theme, since Meera never lets anyone be *her* emergency contact.
4. **MEDIUM, Ep 10.** "Inka decide cheyyaledu" is right, but Meera needs one line of her own vulnerability before it, or the ending is all Karthik's growth. *Fix:* she admits something before handing him the pen, e.g. "Nenu kooda adagaledu. Eppudu."
5. **LOW, Ep 4.** Arjun's confession should cost him something visible (he still wears the watch she gifted?) so the audience feels his loss too.

## What must not be lost
The chair motif (Karthik asleep in Ep 1, Meera in Ep 7). The long silence returning in Ep 10. Arjun as the person who saw her clearly.

# Arjun, Dramaturg

## Verdict
The causal engine — a form written seven months ago by the wrong man — is excellent and mostly airtight. Three holes would make an attentive viewer stop believing: where Karthik was during the dengue night, a dangling doorbell, and a duplicated emergency-contact update. Fix those and the plot holds.

## Issues
1. **HIGH, Ep 9 → 10.** Ep 9 ends on a doorbell, and Ep 10's summary never pays it off ("It's Jyothi at the door? No…"). That is a broken promise. *Fix:* replace the doorbell with an ending we actually pay off (see Ep 10), or say who rang and why.
2. **HIGH, Eps 9 and 10.** Meera updates her emergency contact in the app (Ep 9) and again at reception (Ep 10). The second update has no reason to exist if the first was saved. *Fix:* in Ep 9 she types "Amma" but does **not** save it, and something interrupts her. Ep 10's reception form is the real decision.
3. **HIGH, Ep 3.** If Meera called Karthik in a panic seven months ago, where was he? Either he didn't come, which undercuts "dependable in a crisis", or he came, and then why does Arjun's decision need only a phone call? *Fix:* Karthik arrived at 6 a.m. that day with idli and a phone charger, and Arjun saw him through the ward glass. That is what decided him. Show it in the flashback.
4. **MEDIUM, Ep 6.** Meera walks out on Arjun mid-date with no consequence. *Fix:* Arjun drives her to the bus stand himself. That shows he has genuinely grown and makes Ep 9's "no" harder.
5. **MEDIUM, Ep 7.** Why does Sarala ask for "the girl's" number? *Fix:* plant that Sarala already knows about her ("the one you talk to at 1 a.m.").
6. **LOW, Ep 2.** Hospitals usually ask the patient to confirm the contact at discharge. *Fix:* Jyothi asks Meera to "confirm or change" it; Meera says "later". That also sets up Ep 10.

## What must not be lost
The A. Varma signature reveal (Ep 2). Karthik freezing over his mother's form (Ep 7), a perfect mirror.

# Tara, Retention Specialist

## Verdict
Ep 1 is a near-perfect microdrama opener, and Eps 6-7 are the binge peak. The risk is the two "talky" valleys: Ep 2 (logistics) and Ep 8 (a calm breakup in a car). Endings are varied and mostly strong. The doorbell in Ep 9 is a cheap cliffhanger that isn't paid off.

## Issues
1. **HIGH, Ep 8.** A reasonable conversation in a moving car is dead air on mobile. *Fix:* cold-open on the pen being set down, then move the confrontation somewhere charged: the Warangal bus stand at dawn, with the bus horn forcing a decision. She boards alone.

# Veda, World & Character Guardian

## Verdict
The Hyderabad world is specific and respectful: mothers, muhurthams and a monsoon flyover all feel lived-in. Character integrity holds for Meera and Arjun. Two cultural moments need care so they don't tip into cliché: the lunch and Padma's role.

## Issues
1. **MEDIUM, Ep 9.** A ring at the first family lunch is fast even for an eager family. It reads as melodrama. *Fix:* no ring. Lakshmi brings a box of sweets and a muhurtham list from the purohit. Real, funny and still pressure.
2. **MEDIUM, Ep 9.** Padma "backs" Meera with almost no build-up, having adored Arjun. *Fix:* her support should come from the hurt of not being told about the accident. "Nuvvu naaku cheppaledu ante, ikkada kooda nee manasu cheppatledu" ties her turn to the theme.
3. **LOW, Ep 7.** Meera travelling overnight to his mother's bedside will be read by Sarala, and by the audience, as a declaration. Let Sarala name that gently. It's a strength.
4. **LOW, all episodes.** Keep the Telugu spoken, in the Hyderabad register. Avoid bookish forms in Sarala's lines even though she's a teacher; she's warm, not formal.
5. **LOW, Ep 10.** Arjun leaving for Pune within days is quick. Make it "transfer accepted", effective next month.

## What must not be lost
Sarala's gate. Jyothi's flat kindness. No villains anywhere.

# Kiran, Cold Audience

## Verdict
I was hooked in five seconds and I'd keep watching. But in Ep 2 I'd have no idea who "Arjun Varma" is, so the big reveal would land as "who?" I also didn't understand why Karthik keeps showing up but won't say anything, until Ep 7.

## Issues
1. **HIGH, Ep 2.** The signature reveal only works if I've met Arjun before it. *Fix:* plant him earlier in Ep 2. Padma, on the phone, mentions "Arjun vaalla amma temple lo kalisaru", and Meera's face changes. Then the signature lands.
2. **MEDIUM, Eps 1-5.** I'm told Karthik is "dependable", but I don't know what he does or why he's with her at all. *Fix:* one line or visual about their history (headphones, studio, "two years"), early.
3. **MEDIUM, Ep 9.** "She chose neither" is clear. The folder of voice notes in Tara's fix would make me scream in a good way, but only if Karthik's secret makes sense: show the folder name "work" on screen.
4. **LOW, Ep 10.** I wanted to see Meera say something real, not just make him write. Agree with Maya.

## What must not be lost
"Do you want him to leave?" and the silence. "Someone from work." Those two moments are why I'd share it.

# Verdict

## Executive verdict
This is a strong, coherent outline with an excellent premise engine: a form written by the wrong man. It has a clear thematic question and a no-villain triangle that will divide comments productively. Its weaknesses are structural, not tonal. There is a broken setup at the end of Ep 9 (doorbell) and a redundant second contact update. Arjun is unplanted before the Ep 2 reveal, the dengue night has a Karthik-shaped hole, and Karthik's turn from Ep 8 to Ep 10 is unseen. All are fixable inside the existing ten episodes without adding scenes of exposition. With the required changes below, I'd rate this ready for beats.

## Scorecard
- **Emotional identification: 8.** Meera's pride and silence are instantly recognisable (Ep 1). Karthik goes cold on screen in Ep 5.
- **Character motivation: 7.** Strong for Meera and Arjun. Karthik's fear is explained only in Ep 7 and his turn is missing.
- **Dramatic causality: 6.** The form engine is excellent, but the doorbell, the double update and the dengue-night hole cost points.
- **Retention: 7.** Ep 1 and Eps 6-7 are excellent. Ep 8 is a talky valley.
- **Relationship chemistry: 8.** Small acts of care read well; the pesarattu scene needs compression, not removal.
- **Conflict escalation: 8.** Stakes climb cleanly from an embarrassment to a family lunch to a public declaration.
- **World/canon consistency: 8.** Hyderabad and Telugu family life are specific. The ring is a false note.
- **Cold-viewer clarity: 6.** "Arjun Varma" is unknown at the reveal.

## Rulings on disagreements
- **Ep 8 location (Tara vs the outline's car):** Tara wins. The Warangal bus stand at dawn makes the breakup visual and decisive, and Meera boarding alone is the image of her choosing herself.
- **Ep 9 ending (Arjun the dramaturg: "pay off the doorbell" vs Tara: "replace it"):** Tara's voice-note folder is stronger. It pays off Karthik's secret and gives him the private, costly act Maya says is missing (Maya issue 2). One change solves three critiques.
- **Ep 2 hook (Tara's missed calls):** Partially accepted. Use the cracked screen, but the missed calls are from *after* the hospital called him (11 calls between 9:40 and 10:05 pm). Calls from the night before would contradict the accident timeline.
- **Ep 10 Meera's vulnerability (Maya, Kiran):** Upheld. She must say one true, costly line before handing him the pen.

## Rejected claims
- **Ep 2 discharge procedure (Arjun the dramaturg issue 6) as a HIGH item:** Kept as a small beat ("confirm later"), not a structural change. Hospitals vary, and the beat is useful only as a plant for Ep 10.
- **Ep 4, Arjun wearing her gifted watch (Maya issue 5):** Rejected. It's sentimental and pulls focus from his honesty. His dignity is the cost.

## REQUIRED CHANGES
1. **Ep 2:** Plant Arjun before the reveal. Padma mentions "Arjun vaalla amma" on the phone and Meera reacts. Open on the cracked phone with 11 missed calls from Karthik (9:40–10:05 pm). At discharge Jyothi asks Meera to confirm or change the contact; Meera says "later".
2. **Ep 3:** The flashback includes Karthik arriving at 6 a.m. with idli and a charger, seen by Arjun through the ward glass. That is what decides Arjun. Keep it under 30 seconds.
3. **Ep 5:** Keep "Nee istam", but show Karthik's hurt to the audience only (the pesarattu burns, his hand grips the pan). Reach "someone from work" by 0:50.
4. **Ep 6:** Arjun drives Meera to the bus stand himself after Karthik's call.
5. **Ep 7:** Sarala already knows about "the girl you talk to at 1 a.m." She names Meera's overnight journey for what it is.
6. **Ep 8:** Cold-open on the pen set down. Move the breakup to the Warangal bus stand at dawn; Meera boards alone. Then Padma's lunch call.
7. **Ep 9:** No ring; sweets and a muhurtham list instead. Padma discovers the accident at lunch (the cast), and her support grows out of that hurt. Meera types "Amma" in the app but does not save. Replace the doorbell with the ending: Karthik shares a folder called "work" (two years of her voice notes).
8. **Ep 10:** Remove the doorbell line. Open at the hospital with the cast coming off. Meera says one true line about never asking before she hands Karthik the pen. Arjun's transfer is "effective next month".

## Recommended changes
- Give Karthik's job one visual early (headphones, the studio) in Ep 1 or Ep 3.
- Karthik signs the cast in Ep 10, the first time he's ever written on it.

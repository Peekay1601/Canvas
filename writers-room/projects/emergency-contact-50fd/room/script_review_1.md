# Maya, Emotional Architect

## Verdict
The pages deliver what the outline promised. The care is shown before it is questioned (Ep 1), Karthik's hurt is visible to us even when Meera can't see it (Ep 5), and the final declaration is earned by the gate story (Ep 7) and the voice-note folder (Ep 9). Meera's last line before the pen ("ఇప్పుడు అడుగుతున్నా") is the emotional key, and it lands. One relationship is still under-built: Sarala and Meera bond in Ep 7, then Sarala has no further beat with her until her voice on the phone in Ep 10.

## Corrections
1. **MEDIUM, Ep 8.** Sarala watches Karthik refuse to write Meera's number and says nothing. That's out of character for the woman who asked him to write it, and it wastes the strongest ally Meera has. *Fix:* after "అవసరం లేదు", Sarala turns to Meera: "నీ number నాకు ఇవ్వు. వాడు రాయకపోయినా, నేను రాసుకుంటా." It gives her an active role and sets up Ep 10.

## Protect
The long silences (Ep 1, Ep 10), "Abstract pesarattu", the folder of 214 voice notes, and Padma's "నీ మనసు గురించి నీకే చెప్పుకోలేదు".

# Arjun, Dramaturg

## Verdict
Causality is tight from the form to the reveal to the confession, and Arjun's choices are all motivated. Two continuity slips and one real logic hole remain. The hole is in the finale: Karthik walks into Aarogya reception on the exact morning of the cast removal, and nothing on the page tells us how he knew.

## Corrections
1. **HIGH, Ep 10.** How does Karthik know Meera is at the hospital at that hour? He is in Warangal and they haven't spoken since the bus stand. *Fix:* plant it through Sarala (see Maya's Ep 8 fix). In Ep 10 Karthik says, breathless: "అమ్మ చెప్పింది... ఇవాళ నీ cast తీసేస్తారని." Meera and Sarala have been texting, which also pays off the alliance.
2. **MEDIUM, Ep 8.** In Ep 7 Meera reports "Mild attack, angiogram రేపు". In Ep 8 Karthik says "Angiogram normal". A normal angiogram contradicts "mild attack". *Fix:* "Angiogram అయిపోయింది. Stent అవసరం లేదంట. Medicines చాలు."
3. **LOW, Ep 3.** The flashback has no on-screen marker beyond the scene heading. Viewers won't read scene headings. *Fix:* add a super, "7 MONTHS AGO", on the first flashback shot, and "NOW" on the return to the car.

## Protect
The A. Varma signature (Ep 2), the 6 a.m. idli in the flashback (Ep 3), and Arjun driving her to MGBS (Ep 6).

# Verdict — Rudra, Critic Judge

## Executive verdict
The script is strong, coherent and in voice. The Telugu is natural, the restraint never turns into slowness, and every episode is under the 2:30 cap. One HIGH problem remains: Karthik's unexplained arrival in Ep 10, which three critics flagged independently and which is confirmed on the page. It can be fixed with one planted beat in Ep 8 that also restores Sarala's agency. The medical contradiction in Ep 8 and the unmarked flashback in Ep 3 are cheap to fix and will be noticed. Not approved this round. Three targeted corrections, then one more review.

## Scorecard
- Emotional identification: 9. Meera's pride and her final ask are deeply relatable (Eps 1, 10).
- Character motivation: 8. Every choice is motivated; Sarala goes passive in Ep 8.
- Dramatic causality: 7. A tight chain, broken by Karthik's unexplained arrival in Ep 10.
- Retention: 9. Varied, strong hooks and endings, all under the cap.
- Dialogue: 9. Natural, code-mixed Hyderabad Telugu with distinct voices.
- Conflict escalation: 8. It climbs cleanly from an embarrassment to a family lunch to a public declaration.
- World/canon consistency: 7. The angiogram line contradicts "mild attack".
- Cold-viewer clarity: 8. The flashback needs a marker.

## Rulings
- **Ep 10, how Karthik knows (Arjun the dramaturg, Kiran):** upheld as HIGH. Fix it through Sarala (Maya's Ep 8 beat plus one line in Ep 10). One plant, two payoffs.
- **Ep 8, Sarala's number (Maya, Veda):** upheld. It is the plant for the Ep 10 fix.
- **Ep 8, angiogram (Arjun the dramaturg, Veda):** upheld as a one-line fix.
- **Ep 3, flashback super (Arjun the dramaturg, Kiran):** upheld. It costs nothing.

## Rejected claims

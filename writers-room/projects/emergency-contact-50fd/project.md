## Logline
After a rain-slick scooty accident, a fiercely independent Hyderabad designer finds her no-label situationship at her bedside — because her ex quietly listed him as her emergency contact months ago. Between the man who shows up but won't name it and the man who named it but never showed up, she must decide whether love means settling for half.

## Original idea
Emergency Contact. Premise: After a minor accident, a fiercely independent woman wakes up to find her situationship at the hospital. She never listed him as her emergency contact—her ex did, months earlier, because he realised who she called when life went wrong. The drama: The man who won’t call himself her boyfriend is dependable in a crisis. The ex who offered commitment was emotionally absent. She starts questioning whether she has to settle for either version of love. Opening hook: “Nurse, he’s not my boyfriend.” “Okay. Do you want him to leave?” A long silence. Episode cliffhanger: He stays all night, then introduces her to his mother over the phone as “someone from work.”
